Meniscus Annotator 0.1.0 - third-party notices and source locations

The Windows wrapper preserves the official 3D Slicer 5.12.4 r34645 runtime.
The original runtime's Python .dist-info licences, notices, DLLs and resources
remain present. This directory adds the application and major native-library
licence/notice files. LICENSE_SOURCES.json records the source URL and SHA256 of
newly collected notices; it is an inventory of those copies, not a change to
any upstream licence.

Application source:
  https://github.com/mssai0303/meniscus-annotator (authorised private repository)
  Application licence: MeniscusAnnotator-Apache-2.0.txt

Slicer source and pinned third-party build recipes:
  https://github.com/Slicer/Slicer/tree/4e21c19d8360242d88d6ca2490bf52400441b8e1
  https://github.com/Slicer/Slicer/tree/4e21c19d8360242d88d6ca2490bf52400441b8e1/SuperBuild

Qt 5.15.2 corresponding open-source source trees and release archive:
  https://github.com/qt/qtbase/tree/v5.15.2
  https://github.com/qt/qtwebengine/tree/v5.15.2
  https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/5.15/5.15.2/single/qt-everywhere-src-5.15.2.tar.xz
  https://github.com/qt/qtwebengine-chromium/tree/fb6ab5e483876298235be1c6a6013b426c82b759
  Qt WebEngine's Chromium and third-party source notices are in that source tree
  and the full Qt source release. Qt shared libraries remain dynamically linked
  in the preserved runtime; this wrapper does not replace them or remove their
  resource files. See the supplied Qt licence texts for their terms.

Additional wrapper dependency:
  NiBabel 5.3.2: https://github.com/nipy/nibabel/tree/5.3.2
  Its MIT licence and attribution are included. The typing_extensions notice is
  supplied separately; its existing runtime distribution is retained unchanged.

NSIS installer:
  https://nsis.sourceforge.io/Download
  NSIS-3.13-COPYING.txt contains its component notices and licence terms.

Sources are referenced for inspection and licence/source availability. Jeff's
routine installed annotation workflow needs no source download or build tools.
The original third-party authors retain their copyrights and licences.
